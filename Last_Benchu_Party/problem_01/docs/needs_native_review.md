# Native-speaker review queue

All glossary entries marked `needs_native_check` are unverified hypotheses. Prioritize Hubballi forms for `ಹೆಂಗ`, `ಯಾಕ`, `ಅದ`, contracted verb endings; Mangaluru forms influenced by Tulu/Konkani/Beary (`ಯಾನ್`, `ಇಜ್ಜಿ`, `ಬರ್ಪೆ`, `ಪೋವೆ`, `ಮಲ್ಪು`, `ಎಂಚ`); and Uttara Kannada contracted `-ರ್ತಿ`/`-ತೀ` forms. Validate each with speakers from named towns and have an independent fluent reviewer approve the standard rendering. Do not promote a confidence label based on generated examples.

The separate [public-source research note](public_source_research.md) adds reported North Karnataka/Havigannada forms as review leads. They are not confirmed mappings; in particular, Havigannada refers to a community variety and should not stand in for all Uttara Kannada Kannada. Three Mangaluru examples in `data/public_source_pairs/` have source-supported meaning glosses, but their expanded textbook sentences still need a local fluent reviewer. Seven TV9-derived Mangaluru/Havyaka glossary candidates are also marked for review; Havyaka remains an experimental extra dialect in the app.
