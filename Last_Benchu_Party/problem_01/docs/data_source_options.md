# External Kannada data options (not team-collected)

Reviewed 18 Nov 2026. These resources can support prototyping or help locate contributors, but none of the freely reusable, openly documented sources below establishes the required 100 verified sentence pairs for each target dialect. Do not copy their records into `data/dataset.csv` as `source=collected`. Keep the provider's license, attribution, metadata and access terms with any derived work.

|---|---|---|---|

The dataset card lists a Kannada train/validation/test subset; filter to Karnataka and inspect districts after access. District does not equal a dialect label, and the data may not cover all requested towns or topics. | Best lead for externally sourced natural speech with district metadata. | [OpenSLR SLR126 (IISc-MILE)](https://openslr.org/126/) | About 350 hours of read Kannada speech from 915 speakers, WAV plus UTF-8 transcripts. | Large general Kannada ASR source, but no published town/dialect breakdown. | Direct download; CC BY 2.0. Training and test archives total about 28 GB. |

## Recommended no-travel workflow

1. If pursuing external corpus material, request access to VAANI's *transcription-part* (not the full audio corpus), accept its conditions using your own Hugging Face account, and download only after access is granted. It is a large resource and contains speaker metadata; retain only fields needed for the project and do not publish personal or fine-grained location data.
2. Filter Kannada transcripts to the named districts only where the released metadata supports that. Label the rows `external_corpus` in a separate working file with source, license, dataset version and original record ID. Do not call a district a dialect without speaker/linguistic validation.
3. Generate textbook-Kannada drafts as a separate annotation layer. A second fluent reviewer must check each one. Preserve the original source transcript and track any edits; do not claim the external contributor gave Nudi Setu-specific consent.
4. Ask the hackathon organizers whether externally collected corpus records meet their data collection and consent-form rules. If they require team-run collection, public corpora are supplementary only. A short online consented call or voice-note workflow remains possible without travelling.

## What is still unknown

- Whether VAANI's transcribed Kannada records include enough Kannada speakers from Sirsi/Kumta/Honnavar, Mangaluru and Hubballi-Dharwad to yield 100 usable rows each. Public pages do not provide the required town-by-dialect counts.
- LDC-IL's named broad classes confirm Mangaluru and Dharwad coverage, but the public documentation does not give town-level counts or a distinct Uttara Kannada dialect class. Its read-speech transcripts do not by themselves provide dialect-to-standard conversion pairs, and the default non-commercial undertaking bars using the corpus or derived work in this app without written permission.
- Whether the contest accepts externally sourced records and provider consent in place of team-held consent forms.
- Whether any external corpus includes everyday home/market/school/travel/festival content balanced as required. General corpus totals do not establish topic balance.
